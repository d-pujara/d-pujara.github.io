Company

Mind Light

Making the work community organizations do easier to manage, understand, and trust.

Mind Light started with a simple question: why do organizations doing essential community work have to fight disconnected tools to explain what happened?

We are building a clearer path from the resources a nonprofit receives, to the decisions it makes, to the obligations and evidence that follow, giving teams more time for their work and an honest way to share its results.

Filmmaking taught me to care about whose story gets told. Mind Light goes a step further: keep the record behind the story credible, and keep people in control of the decisions that matter.
