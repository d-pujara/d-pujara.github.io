hello, i'm

DPdish.

I make films, build software for institutions that serve the public, and write about what happens when human care has to travel through complex systems.

My route into technology ran through film sets, four years in construction trailers, and documentary rooms across California. Today I am building Mind Light (mindlight.studio ↗), an operating record designed to keep community organizations' records credible and keep people in control of consequential decisions.
